Unit 1: Ratio & Proportion (Rule & Challenge)

The Golden Rule: Cross Multiply!
$$\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} \iff a \times d = b \times c$$ To check if two ratios form a proportion, multiply the extremes and the means!
🏋️ Quick Check: Are these two ratios proportional?
$$\frac{5}{8} \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{15}{24}$$
Cross multiply extremes and means!
🦊

Unit 1: Scale Drawings (Rule & Challenge)

The Golden Rule & The Trap
Rule: Map Distance \(\times\) Scale = Actual Distance.
The Trap: Watch the units! To convert from cm to km, divide by \(100,000\) (move decimal 5 places left).
🏋️ Quick Check: A map scale is \(1 : 400,000\). Distance on map is \(3\text{ cm}\). What is the real distance in km?
Move the decimal 5 places left!
🐸
Eng Sara T Olama
🟢 Slide 5/21 • Solution Breakdown

Unit 1: Scale Drawings (Answer Reveal)

Answer: 12 km
1) Calculate cm: \(3\text{ cm} \times 400,000 = 1,200,000\text{ cm}\)
2) Convert to km: Jump 5 decimal places left (divide by 100,000) \(\rightarrow 12\text{ km}\).
Teacher Script: "The math is easy, but the units will trick you! 3 cm times 400,000 is 1,200,000 cm. Now, jump 5 decimal places to get kilometers."
Don't fall for unit traps!

Unit 1: Proportional Division (Rule & Challenge)

The Golden Rule: 1 Part Value
Steps: Total Amount \(\div\) Total Parts (Sum of ratio numbers) = Value of 1 Part.
🏋️ Quick Check: Divide 1,200 EGP between Ahmed and Sara in the ratio \(3 : 5\). How much does Sara get?
Find the value of 1 part first!
🐼
Eng Sara T Olama
🟢 Slide 7/21 • Solution Breakdown

Unit 1: Proportional Division (Answer Reveal)

Answer: 750 EGP
1) Sum of parts: \(3 + 5 = 8\text{ total parts}\)
2) Value of 1 part: \(1,200 \div 8 = 150\text{ EGP}\)
3) Sara's share (\(5\text{ parts}\)): \(5 \times 150 = 750\text{ EGP}\)
Teacher Script: "Add the ratio numbers: 3 plus 5 is 8 parts total. Divide 1,200 by 8. That's 150 per part. Sara gets 5 parts, so 5 times 150."
750 EGP for Sara!

Unit 1: Percentages (Rule & Challenge)

The Fast Multiplier Trick
Increase of \(x\%\): Multiply by \(\left(1 + \frac{x}{100}\right)\)
Decrease of \(x\%\): Multiply by \(\left(1 - \frac{x}{100}\right)\)
🏋️ Quick Check: A phone costs 2,000 EGP. It gets a 15% discount. What is the new price?
15% off means you pay 85%!
📱
Eng Sara T Olama
🟢 Slide 9/21 • Solution Breakdown

Unit 1: Percentages (Answer Reveal)

Answer: 1,700 EGP
Discount of 15% \(\rightarrow\) Remaining percentage = \(100\% - 15\% = 85\% = 0.85\)
New Price: \(2000 \times 0.85 = 1,700\text{ EGP}\)
Teacher Script: "Don't calculate the discount and subtract—that takes too long! If there is a 15% discount, you are paying 85% of the price. Multiply 2000 by 0.85."
Smart shortcuts save exam time!

Unit 1: Sets & Venn Diagrams (Rule & Challenge)

The Golden Rule: Intersection First!
Always fill in \(n(A \cap B)\) first!
Formula: \(n(A \cup B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A \cap B)\)
🏋️ Quick Check: In a class of 35 students, 20 play football, 18 play basketball, and 8 play both. How many play neither?
Subtract the overlap!
⚽
Eng Sara T Olama
🟢 Slide 11/21 • Solution Breakdown

Unit 1: Sets & Venn Diagrams (Answer Reveal)

Answer: 5 Students
1) Football only: \(20 - 8 = 12\)
2) Basketball only: \(18 - 8 = 10\)
3) Total playing at least one: \(12 + 10 + 8 = 30\)
4) Neither: \(35 - 30 = 5\text{ students}\).
Teacher Script: "The intersection is the 8 who play both. Subtract 8 from 20 (12 football only) and 8 from 18 (10 basketball only). Total = 30. Class total = 35. So, 5 play neither."
Venn Diagrams solved cleanly!
