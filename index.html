Unit 1: Ratio & Proportion

The Golden Rule: Cross Multiply!
$$\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} \implies a \times d = b \times c$$
🏋️ Quick Check: Are these ratios proportional?
\(\frac{5}{8}\) and \(\frac{15}{24}\)
Cross multiply extremes and means!
🦊
🟢 Slide 3/19 • Solution Breakdown Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 1: Ratio & Proportion (Solution)

Answer: Yes! Proportional
$$5 \times 24 = 120$$ $$8 \times 15 = 120$$ Both products equal 120, so the ratios form a proportion!
Teacher Script: "If you want to check if two ratios make a proportion, just cross-multiply. Multiply the extremes and the means. If they are equal, it's a proportion."
120 = 120! Great job!

Unit 1: Scale Drawings

The Golden Rule & Trap
Rule: Map Distance \(\times\) Scale = Actual Distance.
The Trap: To convert cm to km, divide by 100,000 (move decimal 5 places left).
🏋️ Quick Check: Map scale is \(1:400,000\). Distance on map is \(3\text{ cm}\). What is actual distance in km?
Watch decimal places!
🐸
🟢 Slide 5/19 • Solution Breakdown Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 1: Scale Drawings (Solution)

Answer: 12 km
1) \(3\text{ cm} \times 400,000 = 1,200,000\text{ cm}\)
2) Move decimal 5 places left \(\implies 12\text{ km}\).
Teacher Script: "The math is easy, but the units will trick you. 3 cm times 400,000 is 1,200,000 cm. Now, jump 5 decimal places to get kilometers."
Spot on! 12 km!

Unit 1: Proportional Division

The Golden Rule
Find value of 1 part first!
Total Amount \(\div\) Total Parts (Sum of ratios) = Value of 1 Part.
🏋️ Quick Check: Divide 1,200 EGP between Ahmed and Sara in ratio \(3:5\). How much does Sara get?
3 + 5 = 8 parts total!
🐼
🟢 Slide 7/19 • Solution Breakdown Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 1: Proportional Division (Solution)

Answer: 750 EGP
1) Total Parts: \(3 + 5 = 8\)
2) Value of 1 Part: \(1,200 \div 8 = 150\text{ EGP}\)
3) Sara's Share (\(5\text{ parts}\)): \(5 \times 150 = 750\text{ EGP}\).
Teacher Script: "Add the ratio numbers: 3 plus 5 is 8 parts total. Divide 1,200 by 8. That's 150 per part. Sara gets 5 parts, so 5 times 150."
750 EGP calculated!

Unit 1: Percentages

The Golden Rule
For increase: multiply by \(\left(1 + \frac{x}{100}\right)\)
For decrease: multiply by \(\left(1 - \frac{x}{100}\right)\)
🏋️ Quick Check: A phone costs 2,000 EGP. It gets a 15% discount. What is the new price?
Multiply 2000 by 0.85!
📱
🟢 Slide 9/19 • Solution Breakdown Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 1: Percentages (Solution)

Answer: 1,700 EGP
Paying percentage: \(100\% - 15\% = 85\% = 0.85\)
New Price: \(2000 \times 0.85 = 1,700\text{ EGP}\).
Teacher Script: "Don't calculate the discount and subtract—that takes too long! If there is a 15% discount, you are paying 85% of the price. Multiply 2000 by 0.85."
Fast multiplier trick!

Unit 1: Sets & Venn Diagrams

The Golden Rule
Fill in the intersection first!
Formula: \(n(A \cup B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A \cap B)\)
🏋️ Quick Check: In a class of 35 students, 20 play football, 18 play basketball, and 8 play both. How many play neither?
Subtract the overlap!
⚽
🟢 Slide 11/19 • Solution Breakdown Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 1: Sets & Venn Diagrams (Solution)

Answer: 5 Students
1) Football only = \(20 - 8 = 12\)
2) Basketball only = \(18 - 8 = 10\)
3) At least one sport = \(12 + 10 + 8 = 30\)
4) Neither = \(35 - 30 = 5\text{ students}\).
Teacher Script: "The intersection is the 8 who play both. Subtract 8 from 20 (12 football only) and 8 from 18 (10 basketball only). Total who play at least one = 12 + 10 + 8 = 30. The class has 35. So, 5 play neither."
Venn Diagram solved!

Unit 1: Integers & Rational Numbers

The Golden Rule
Same signs = Positive. Different signs = Negative.
Rational numbers: fraction \(\frac{a}{b}\) where \(b \neq 0\). (\(\pi\) & \(\sqrt{2}\) are NOT rational).
🏋️ Quick Check: Calculate: $$-6 \times (-4) + (-10)$$
-6 × -4 = +24!
🤖
🟢 Slide 13/19 • Solution Breakdown Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 1: Integers & Rational Numbers (Solution)

Answer: 14
1) Multiply negatives: \(-6 \times (-4) = +24\)
2) Add negative 10: \(24 + (-10) = 14\).
Teacher Script: "Negative times negative is positive, so that's 24. Then add negative 10. 24 minus 10 is 14."
Signs mastered!

Unit 2: Algebra: Expressions & Formulas

The Golden Rule
You can ONLY add/subtract Like Terms (same variable & power).
🏋️ Quick Check 1: Simplify \(8x + 3 - 2x + 7\)
🏋️ Quick Check 2: If \(y = 4\), what is \(3y^2\)?
Careful: Exponent first!
🚀
🟢 Slide 15/19 • Solution Breakdown Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 2: Algebra Expressions (Solution)

Answer: 1: 6x+10 | 2: 48
1) \((8x - 2x) + (3 + 7) = 6x + 10\)
2) \(3 \times (4^2) = 3 \times 16 = 48\). (NOT \(12^2\))!
Teacher Script: "For the first one, group the x's and the numbers. 8x minus 2x is 6x. 3 plus 7 is 10. Answer: 6x + 10. For the second, it's 3 times (4 squared). 4 squared is 16. 3 times 16 is 48. NOT 12 squared!"
Order of operations rule!
🧠
🟢 Slide 16/19 • Unit 2 Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 2: Linear Equations

Inverse Operations
Do the opposite operation to isolate variable!
Get rid of addition/subtraction first, then multiplication/division.
🏋️ Quick Check: Solve for \(x\): $$4x - 9 = 15$$
Add 9, then divide by 4!
⚖️
🟢 Slide 17/19 • Solution Breakdown Eng Sara T Olama

Unit 2: Linear Equations (Solution)

Answer: x = 6
1) Add 9 to both sides: \(4x = 15 + 9 \implies 4x = 24\)
2) Divide by 4: \(x = \frac{24}{4} = 6\).
Teacher Script: "The x is multiplied by 4, then 9 is subtracted. Work backwards. The opposite of subtract 9 is add 9. 15 plus 9 is 24. Now we have 4x equals 24. The opposite of multiply by 4 is divide by 4. x equals 6."
Perfect balance!
